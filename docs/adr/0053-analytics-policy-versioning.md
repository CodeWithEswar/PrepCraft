Status: Accepted · 2026-10-26

Publish `analytics-policy-v1` and `timing-analytics-v1` in every relevant evidence projection. Eligibility, denominators, thresholds or comparison changes require policy and fixture changes. Historical source policy/version is preserved for simulation grouping.
