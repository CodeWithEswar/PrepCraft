# Analytics overview

The Preparation brief surfaces a supported complete-period timing comparison or an honest observation/empty state. Comparisons require equal local-calendar-day periods, sufficient eligible observations and one difficulty, mode and session type. They do not establish causation.

The constraint finder selects a canonically attributed topic with at least five eligible attempts and supported target variance, assistance or current overdue Revision evidence. It displays the reasons and source policy; it does not generate a hidden universal score. Unmapped topics and capped source ranges cannot become recommendations.

The overview combines timing, duration/outcome relationships, time by topic, independence/assistance and difficulty evidence. The decision rail uses qualitative states supplied by the current Mastery engine, a preparation calendar and source actions. It does not invent a readiness ring or streak.

Calendar-clipped focus and full attempt durations are different measurements. The period narrative labels those sources separately. Current Revision due counts and current Mastery windows are explicitly distinct from a custom historical Analytics range.
