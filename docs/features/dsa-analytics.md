# DSA evidence

The DSA view consumes canonical Mastery states rather than duplicating its formula. The source window, policy, evidence confidence and attempt count accompany each state. Clicking an area opens topic evidence. A custom Analytics period does not reconstruct historical Mastery.

Independent, assisted and unsolved outcomes come from committed attempts. Actual hint/cue use prevents a reported independent solve from being treated as independent. These are self-reported coding outcomes; objective correctness requires a server-evaluated Learning binding.

Difficulty rows preserve insufficient evidence. Rates require five eligible attempts; median time requires three eligible timings. Recorded pattern counts have no inferred assignment from question titles. Revision recall outcomes and interval transitions come from the shared Revision engine; initial practice and early rehearsal remain separate from retention evidence.

No prerequisite edges or causal weakness chains are invented. The optional Topics relationship map uses explicit recorded topic/pattern pairs only.
