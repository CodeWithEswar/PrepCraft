# Explainable Weaknesses

A finding requires Moderate or Strong evidence confidence; sparse or stale data is reported as a gap rather than proof of poor performance. Rules are deterministic: weighted initial independent rate below .4; at least two reviews with independent retention below .67; weighted hint dependence at least .5; at least two independently solved Interview attempts with median duration above 1.2× target; repeated self-reported recognition/edge-case/complexity mistakes across at least two distinct learning identities; or repeated confidence/outcome mismatch.

Each finding carries a stable reason code, severity, confidence, actual count, explanation and owning-feature action. Major/minor assistance remain distinct. Mistake rules consume immutable attempt tags and explicitly say self-reported; no Mistake Journal record or automated correctness classification is fabricated.

Weakness Constellation is a restrained hierarchy for observations sharing an area. Its connections do not claim causation. A severe-looking single result does not outrank well-supported evidence. Gaps have their own view and do not become zero mastery.
