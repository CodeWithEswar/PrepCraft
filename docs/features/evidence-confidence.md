# Evidence Confidence

Confidence in the evidence is separate from the user's reported confidence and from mastery level. No meaningful attempts means Insufficient. Fewer than three initial attempts, fewer than two distinct learning identities, fewer than two workspace-local active days, or latest evidence older than 45 days means Limited.

Strong confidence requires at least eight initial attempts, three distinct identities, four active days, a 14-day evidence span, two real revision reviews, two Medium/Hard observations, and latest evidence within 14 days. Otherwise sufficiently varied current evidence is Moderate. Easy-only volume cannot create Strong confidence.

Sparse evidence is Unassessed rather than Needs Work. Historical strong performance can retain its qualitative level while evidence confidence becomes Limited; a stale-evidence gap asks for fresh practice. Each missing dimension remains null and names the observations required. There is no confidence percentage or scientific retention claim.
