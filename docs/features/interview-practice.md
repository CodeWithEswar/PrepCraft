# Interview practice

Interview mode is structured self-practice under a countdown. Policy v1 forbids pause, permits at most two supplied hints for the session and ends timing at its deadline. It provides an optional guidance rail: Understand, Clarify, Approach, Implement, Test, Review. Users may move between guidance phases freely; they are not an inferred performance assessment.

The collapsible communication checklist records self-reported assumptions, approach explanation, complexity discussion, edge-case testing and trade-offs. It never claims PrepCraft listened to or assessed speech. Phase/checklist mutations are authorized and persisted; captured phase events can appear in the attempt trace.

The timer uses restrained Sienna emphasis at attention/closing thresholds. It does not flash, sound alarms or announce every second. Manual finish requires confirmation and stops timing before debrief. Returning after the deadline restores the expired state and saved work rather than restarting.
