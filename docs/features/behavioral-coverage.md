# Behavioral coverage

Coverage states are qualitative and explainable. No story: no active mapping. Draft coverage: only draft/developing stories are mapped. Needs practice: a practice-ready story exists but lacks qualifying recent evidence. Ready: a practice-ready story has meaningful completed practice within 21 days and latest self-confidence at least 3 and a successful self-reported retelling. Strong: at least two meaningful practices on two workspace-local dates, latest successful self-reported retelling with confidence at least 4, no cues in the latest practice and practice within 14 days.

Meaningful means a committed Behavioral reflection, completed Execution and at least 15 focused seconds. Retired stories do not contribute. Counts and states derive from source data at read time; no parallel persisted score exists. Self-confidence and cue use cannot establish interview success or independently judge narrative quality. Empty evidence remains an explicit gap.

The coverage matrix shows mappings across up to five recent filtered stories. Mobile displays labeled competency cards, not a clipped horizontal matrix. The right context rail becomes a keyboard-accessible modal drawer below 1200px.
