# Consistency analytics

The calendar and rhythm strip describe recorded preparation, not personal discipline. Explicit rest days are neutral; missing plans are not classified as missed sessions. A date button opens a readable explanation of recorded focus, activities and shared plan status.

Focus uses closed intervals from terminal, non-invalidated sessions. Intervals are clipped to workspace-local dates and merged to avoid overlap. DST days can be 23 or 25 hours. Completed unplanned sessions count. Task estimates are never converted into actual study time.

Plan counts are workspace-shared, whereas tracked focus is personal. Cancelled, deferred and rest-day tasks are excluded from the applicable denominator. Carryover lineage and move events are distinct from completion. Historical planning counts describe currently stored task states; no missing move reason or original plan snapshot is reconstructed.

Difficulty, practice-mode, question-type and session-type filters have no authoritative mapping to every planned task. Under those filters, planning counts remain unavailable/null, not zero. Completion fractions need five applicable tasks. Weekly charts use separate labeled task-count and duration axes, with full tables available.
