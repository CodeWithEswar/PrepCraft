# Readiness evidence policy

readiness-evidence-v1 is a deterministic projection, not a hiring prediction or a second Mastery score. It consumes the existing 90-day Mastery snapshot and canonical interview analytics. Missing sources are unavailable; sparse evidence is insufficient.

DSA, language and CS dimensions retain required-topic coverage, source confidence, recency and next links. A supported signal requires at least five attempts across two observed required topics, complete required coverage, recent evidence within fourteen days, stable/strong topic levels and adequate source confidence. A weak topic makes the dimension needs_focus.

OA, behavioral and mock dimensions expose actual record counts and evidence limitations. Behavioral self-practice is labeled self_reported. Project discussion is unavailable. Role weighting and an overall percentage remain unavailable until a complete versioned target contract exists. No synthetic score is stored.
