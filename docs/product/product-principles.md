# PrepCraft Product Principles

## 1. Evidence Over Vanity Scores
Preparation is not a gamified streak or an arbitrary percentage. PrepCraft captures tangible evidence: code written, patterns recognized, edge cases missed, trade-offs articulated, and real interview rounds logged.

## 2. Show the Next Useful Action
Candidates are easily overwhelmed by thousands of LeetCode questions. PrepCraft focuses on the immediate highest-leverage task: a due revision interval, a gap identified from yesterday’s attempt, or an upcoming interview round.

## 3. Deliberate Practice & Reflection
Solving a problem without understanding *why* a particular approach was chosen leads to fragile recall. PrepCraft treats mistake classification (logic, complexity, edge case, pattern gap) as a first-class citizen alongside the code.

## 4. Spaced Recall, Not Cramming
Retention decays on a predictable Ebbinghaus curve. PrepCraft's revision engine schedules deliberate spaced revisits to transform temporary solutions into permanent intuition.

## 5. Real Interview Tracking (Zero Synthetic Mocks)
PrepCraft is a tracker and preparation workspace for real software engineering opportunities. We do not provide AI mock interviewers or fake video interviews. We provide structured tracking for Online Assessments, technical rounds, behavioral stories, project deep-dives, and offer evaluations.

## 6. Strict Data Ownership & Workspace Isolation
Preparation notes, code attempts, and application histories belong exclusively to the candidate's workspace. Cross-workspace leakage is strictly prevented at both the application and database (RLS) layers.
